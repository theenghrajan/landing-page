What we do

Specialized care for fine and delicate rugs

Fine rugs should never be cleaned on the floor of your home. Pretreatment and washing are done under controlled conditions in our facility, where each piece gets the handling its material calls for.

01

Area Rug Cleaning

Careful, thorough cleaning for a wide range of rug types and fibers, including:

  • Wool, cotton, silk, and viscose
  • Tufted and hooked rugs
  • Needlepoints and kilims
  • Bordered rugs
02

Oriental, Silk & Antique Rugs

Handmade and machine-made Oriental rugs, silk rugs, and antique pieces made of delicate, hard-to-replace fibers.

Before washing, we identify each rug’s fibers, dyes, and condition, and test for colorfastness. Pet and wine stains receive special pretreatment to give them the best chance of a good result.

03

Rug Repair & Restoration

Our staff of weavers handles restoration work from everyday wear to heirloom and museum-quality pieces:

  • Fringe end replacement
  • Edge and dye restoration
  • Reweaving worn or damaged areas
  • Moth damage restoration
  • Hooked and needlepoint rug restoration
Our process

How we care for your rug

These steps apply to most area rugs. Some pieces need special handling, which we decide once your rug is in our facility.

  1. Inspection and stain identification

    Every rug is inspected to identify fibers, construction, and any areas of concern before cleaning begins.

  2. Dry soil and allergen removal

    Our soil separation system with HEPA filtration removes embedded dust, grit, and pet hair before any water touches the rug.

  3. Colorfast testing

    Dyes are tested before washing. Not every rug can be wet cleaned, and we’ll tell you if yours can’t.

  4. Gentle washing and fresh-water rinse

    Most wool Oriental rugs are washed front and back in a full-immersion system with a green-formulated solution, then rinsed to leave no soapy residue.

  5. Climate-controlled drying

    Rugs dry in dehumidified, climate-controlled conditions, then fringes are washed and groomed separately.

  6. Final inspection and delivery

    Each rug is groomed, inspected, rolled, and wrapped for protection. Our staff can pick up and deliver for you.

What to expect

Every rug is different

A rug’s fibers, dyes, age, and condition all shape what cleaning and restoration can achieve. Some stains, odors, and signs of wear respond well to treatment; others depend on how the rug was made and what it has been through.

That’s why we start with an inspection and give you an honest assessment of the best approach for your piece, before any work begins.
